# Sweep 1: artist and gallery stores (fetched 2026-10-23)

Method note: WebFetch returns a summarized text capture, not screenshots. Layout, modal-vs-inline behavior and sold badges were often NOT visible. "Not visible" below means not in the capture, not proven absent. Blocked or dead: Artsy (403), Saatchi Art (403), Uprise (DNS fail), Kehinde Wiley (empty), Kasmin (stale 2018 snapshot), lauraowens.com (domain for sale), deep product/collection pages mostly 404 or empty. Gap: no clear example of an inquire form or sold badge was captured; a visual check of a few sites in a real browser is recommended before final design.

## Sites opened (16 usable)

1. Tappan Collective, tappancollective.com. Shopify (cdn/shop, Shop Pay). Hero: 4-slide carousel, label plus "By <artist>", no headline; "Featured in Architectural Digest" bar. Nav: Collect (by medium), Artists, The Edits (mood collections), Journal, Info, Art Style Quiz. Buy: online checkout, plus art advisory and trade program. Prices shown ("From $X"). Sold: not visible. Story: Featured Artist tiles with portraits, Journal collector profiles. Steal: cards rotate artwork image to styled room view; style quiz and mood "Edits" for non-expert collectors.
2. Partnership Editions, partnershipeditions.com. Shopify (StudioSmall). Hero: full-width video, "ALL FOR ART ART FOR ALL", single CTA "SHOP ALL ARTWORKS", founder quote. Nav: Shop Art, Collections, Artists, Advisory, Journal. Prices in GBP on cards. Sold: not visible. Steal: one decisive CTA, plus an advisory and trade block on the home page (a designer lane).
3. James Niehues, jamesniehues.com. Shopify-style. Hero: room-scene photo, "Legendary Ski Artist James Niehues", CTAs "Get the book" / "Shop art prints", LA Times quote right under. Nav only 4 items: Art Prints, Originals, The Book, About. Screen 2: three story blocks, press logos, category tiles, best-sellers. Prices "From $X". Steal: identity headline plus third-party press quote in screen 1; art shown in a room.
4. Jen Stark, jenstark.com. Squarespace. Closest aesthetic peer (kaleidoscopic color). No hero text, signature logo; nav: 3-D, 2-D, public art, animation, glass, merch, about, news. Screen 2 is a captioned image grid (title, year, materials, size), 146 pages. No prices, inquire or sold on the portfolio. Steal: pure portfolio mode with museum-style captions; shop separated at /store.
5. Nick Mayer Art, nickmayerart.com. Shopify. Hero slider "MARINE BIOLOGIST PAINTED ART / CONNECTING YOU WITH NATURE" (identity in one line). Nav: SHOP (Featured, Original Paintings, Prints...), NICK'S STORY, PRAISE; "Commission a Painting" link. Prices shown ("From"). Testimonial and press quotes plus "As Seen In" logos. Steal: one-line identity hero, a PRAISE page, commission link in nav.
6. Rossetti Art, rossettiart.com. Platform not named. "Buy Original Paintings Online"; shipping/returns banner; "Quick add" cards; strike-through prices; FAQ block on collection page; social proof "8 people viewed this week". Steal: FAQ on the collection page ("is it hand-painted / how do I choose"). Mass-market feel, not a tone model.
7. Hunt Slonem, huntslonem.com. Squarespace. Hero: 6-slide painting carousel with captions (title, year, medium, size). Nav: Artist, Artworks, Exhibitions, Press, Contact. No inquire, price or sold visible; contact only. Steal: Exhibitions and Press as top-level nav (credibility for galleries).
8. Andy Okay, andyokay.com. Shopify. Hero: charity-discount headline, "SHOP BEST SELLERS". Stats strip (226 artists, 213k sold, reviews). Has a "just sold out" section and "Almost Gone" nav, urgency copy "sell out fast", and "Join the Club" for early access on sold-out items. Steal: sold-out items route to a next step (waitlist) instead of a dead end. Closest match to Angela's sold-to-commission idea.
9. Art Images Gallery (AU), artimagesgallery.com.au. Shopify. Home leads with Current Exhibition blocks (4 shows, dates, "View exhibition"). Nav: SHOP ART, ARTISTS, EXHIBITIONS, SERVICES, ABOUT US. 10. Singulart, singulart.com. Marketplace. Hero "Buy art from the world's best artists", CTA "Start discovering". Trust strip: 30-day returns, insured shipping, certificate of authenticity, 4.9/5 from 20k reviews. Price-band browsing (under $500 to over $20,000). A "Just sold" category page. Steal: trust strip near the CTA; "Just sold" as social proof.

Nav: Browse By (medium, genre, size, orientation), Curated Collections by Room/Theme/Color/Mood, About, Trade Program. "Starting at $35." 13. Ashley Longshore, ashleylongshore.com. Shopify (Stray Kat Studio). Nearest "personality painter". Rotating hero, headline "WELCOME TO THE LONGSHORE UNIVERSE", CTA "Check Out My Paintings". 14. Lisa Congdon, lisacongdon.com. Shopify. Hero "LONG LIVE THE TENDER-HEARTED", CTAs "SHOP PRINTS" and "MEET LISA" (story is a first-class CTA). Originals sold as "drops", "one-of-a-kind, signed and dated on the backs". Steal: paired CTAs (buy plus meet the artist); drops language for scarcity.

16. Camille Walala, camillewalala.com. Custom (Next.js?). Ultra-minimal nav: About, Projects, Shop; Shop points to a mailing-list page. Steal: minimalism only; shop-as-mailing-list is a friction risk.

## Key findings
- Almost no artist-direct site shows "Sold" prominently; marketplaces use a separate "Just sold" page or section as social proof.
- Sold-out items convert best with a next step (Andy Okay waitlist; Longshore Commissions). Supports "sold, submit commission inquiry".
- No-price painting sites (Longshore, Slonem, Stark) route to Contact, Commissions or Appointment; none showed an inline form in the capture, so D4 inline dropdown is a differentiator.
- Heroes that state identity in one line ("Legendary Ski Artist...", "Marine biologist painted art") plus third-party press in screen 1 suit visitors who know the name but not the story.
- Pair CTAs: one browse/buy, one "Meet <artist>" (Congdon).
- Trade/advisory is top-level nav at Tappan, 20x200, Rise Art, Partnership Editions, Art Images: precedent for a "For designers & galleries" lane.
- Exhibitions and Press as nav items (Slonem, Art Images) carry credentials.
- Browse by color/room/mood (20x200, Tappan Edits) suits color-led work.
- Trust strip near the CTA: certificate of authenticity, insured shipping, returns (Singulart, Rise).
- Art in a room scene (Niehues hero, Tappan card hover) helps designers and collectors imagine it.
- Museum captions (title, year, medium, size) as at Stark and Slonem read as serious and fit the portfolio page.
