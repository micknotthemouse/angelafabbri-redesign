Meet Angela

Self-taught. Soul-driven. Painting in reverse.

Angela Fabbri took her first commission at sixteen and has painted professionally since 2017. She has exhibited at the 2019 Florence Biennale, held a solo exhibition in Marfa, Texas, and shown at consecutive Miami Art Weeks; Baccarat, Thrillist and Dos Equis have invited her to paint live. Her work lives in hundreds of private and corporate collections.

“California is where the dreamer in me was born, Mississippi taught me to slow down, and in Texas I learned the value in that stillness.”
