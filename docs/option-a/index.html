Meet Angela

California born, Mississippi raised and Texas based.

Angela Fabbri is a self-taught painter who received her first commission at 16 and has painted professionally since 2017. Her backwards text-based work has shown at the Florence Biennale, in a solo exhibition in Marfa and at Miami Art Week, and hangs in hundreds of private and corporate collections. She has painted live for Baccarat, Thrillist and Dos Equis, and opened a 2019 pop-up gallery in Houston’s River Oaks District.
