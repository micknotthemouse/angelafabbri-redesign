Artist statement

Shaped by life across California, Mississippi, and now Texas, I’m self-taught and soul-driven. I’ve spent my life following the pull of creativity. From an early commission at sixteen to showing in global art capitals like Florence and Miami, I’ve trusted the process.

I paint in reverse—and not just for the fun of making people tilt their heads. My art plays with perception. For me, art is not just visual; it's experiential. Through backwards text and vibrant color, I invite viewers to slow down, look again, and be fully present in that moment. My process is intuitive, but intentional. I like to mix beauty with meaning, and polish with play. Whether I’m painting live for a crowd or layering paint in my studio, I’m always chasing that electric moment when a piece clicks—when someone connects with it, even if they can’t quite explain why.

My work lives in hundreds of private and corporate collections and has been embraced by major institutions, including MOCA and the Whitney. These recognitions fuel my mission to make art that captivates and connects. Through vibrant palettes and layered meanings, I seek to transform everyday spaces into portals of curiosity and joy. At the end of the day, I’m just here to make the world a little more colorful, a little more curious, and a lot more fun to look at.

–

Bio

California born, Mississippi raised, and Texas based, Angela Fabbri is a self-taught artist. Internationally recognized for her backwards text-based work, she has earned praise from press, celebrity and international collectors. With her contemporary realism and bold use of color, she has achieved milestones, including exhibiting at the prestigious 2019 Florence Biennale in Italy, a solo exhibition in the artistic haven of Marfa, TX, and consecutive participation in the largest art week in the world, Art Basel Week Miami.

Her creative talent manifested early on when, at the age of 16, she received her first commission. With over 25 years creating, Fabbri has been a professional artist since 2017, and her work can be found in 100s of private and corporate collections.

The Museum of Contemporary Art (MOCA) in Los Angeles recognized her talent in 2017, selecting her work for a group exhibition in Houston, a gesture echoed again in 2018 by the assistant curator for the Whitney in New York. Live painting events for Baccarat, Thrillist, and Dos Equis further established her presence in an interactive way.

In 2019, Fabbri expanded her engagement with art enthusiasts by establishing a popup gallery at Houston's luxury shopping destination, The River Oaks District. With iconic neighbors, Harry Winston, Hermes and others, Fabbri's kaleidoscopic world of color became a captivating destination for three exhilarating months.

Fabbri’s work has been featured in several publications including, Vogue, The Washington Post, The Houston Chronicle, Modern Luxury, Paper City, The Book of Lists, and The Round Top Show Guide. She looks forward to continuing her passionate journey in this wonderful world of color.

Works

In conversation

Inspiration comes to me from all places at all times...it could be the color of a vine growing on the side of a building, a word someone says during a dinner conversation, or a lyric from a song. Each body of work or individual painting has its own specific inspiration or story I'm trying to tell, but one underlying theme throughout my work is that time is our most valuable currency. We can't work harder for more, we can't save it up, and it doesn't compound. Some of my paintings are bolder in conveying this, like my clocks with the backwards message "Invest Wisely" and some more subtle with layers of detail that you would only see if you stood still to enjoy the moment.

One of the most surprising moments I’ve had was at my opening in Round Top Texas during the Design Show when a viewer complemented my work, specifically liking that I did my words backwards so that it forces you to slow down and be in the moment. I was so pleasantly shocked that she understood exactly what I was doing and why....explaining it to me as if she were the creator. All being the first time she had been exposed to my work. That feeling, knowing that the viewer "got it" organically was so rewarding.

In conversation

Joy, laughter, confusion, curiosity, accomplishment, are all just some of the feels I hope to evoke in my viewers. At first glance, the viewer is drawn to my work because the color or subject grabs their attention. From there I want to spark their curiosity with my backwards text, pulling them in to focus a little more intently because it's harder to read backwards...it forces you to slow down. At that moment, they have been on an effortless journey, guiding them to a fully present place.

I continuously explore my techniques, working to loosen and refine in all the right ways. I want my work to have an effortless but intentional feel with the different techniques that I combine. Loose, messy stencil paint that bleeds outside the lines against sharp, crisp edges from an opposing detail. Challenging myself with different subjects, unexpected color combinations, asking myself how I can add another layer to the story I'm telling. Life experiences are so transformative. They provide perspective, great levels of joy, sadness, appreciation, adventure, accomplishment...all stories I want to tell through my work.

Commissions

Initial Consultation: Begin by discussing your desired size, color palette, and the specific space where the artwork will be displayed. Angela encourages sharing inspiration images or fabric swatches to ensure the piece aligns with your environment.

Design Proposal: Angela will provide a design concept or mock-up based on your preferences. This stage allows for adjustments to ensure the artwork meets your expectations.

Agreement and Deposit: Once the design is approved, a contract is established outlining the project's scope, timeline, and pricing. A deposit is required to initiate the work.

Creation Process: Angela will commence the creation of the artwork, keeping you informed with progress updates. Your feedback is welcomed to ensure the piece evolves according to your vision.

Completion and Delivery: Upon completion, the final piece is delivered to your location of preference.
