How it works

From first conversation to your wall

  1. Consultation

    Initial Consultation: Begin by discussing your desired size, color palette, and the specific space where the artwork will be displayed. Angela encourages sharing inspiration images or fabric swatches to ensure the piece aligns with your environment.

  2. Proposal and agreement

    Design Proposal: Angela will provide a design concept or mock-up based on your preferences. This stage allows for adjustments to ensure the artwork meets your expectations.

    Agreement and Deposit: Once the design is approved, a contract is established outlining the project's scope, timeline, and pricing. A deposit is required to initiate the work.

  3. Creation and delivery

    Creation Process: Angela will commence the creation of the artwork, keeping you informed with progress updates. Your feedback is welcomed to ensure the piece evolves according to your vision.

    Completion and Delivery: Upon completion, the final piece is delivered to your location of preference.
