ANGELA

A journal in paint.

California is where the dreamer in me was born, Mississippi taught me to slow down, and in Texas I learned the value in that stillness.

Angela took her first commission at 16 and has painted professionally since 2017. Her contemporary realism, kaleidoscopic color and backwards-lettered Inversion series hang in hundreds of private and corporate collections. She has live-painted for Baccarat, Thrillist and Dos Equis, and opened a 2019 pop-up gallery in Houston’s River Oaks District.
