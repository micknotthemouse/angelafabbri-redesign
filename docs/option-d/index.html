02Artist

02.1 · Meet AngelaSelf-taught. Texas based. Backwards on purpose.

Angela Fabbri took her first commission at sixteen and has painted professionally since 2017. Self-taught, California born, Mississippi raised and Texas based, she is known for backwards text and contemporary realism in bold color. Baccarat, Thrillist and Dos Equis have invited her to paint live.

  • Age 16First commission.
  • 2017 · HoustonSelected by MOCA Los Angeles for a group exhibition.
  • 2018Selected again by the assistant curator for the Whitney, New York.
  • 2019 · FlorenceFlorence Biennale.
  • 2019 · HoustonThree-month popup gallery at River Oaks District, beside Harry Winston and Hermès.
